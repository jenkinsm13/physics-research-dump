## Geometric Regimes

The three lepton generations map to three geometric regimes relative to the correlation length `omega`:

- **Finite** (`i < omega`): Electron -- deep within a correlation domain, stable
- **Critical** (`i ~ omega`): Muon -- at the domain boundary, inherently unstable
- **Transfinite** (`i > omega`): Tau -- non-local resonance across domains

This elegantly explains **why there are exactly three generations** of leptons.

## Equal Generation Spacing

A key structural finding: the ordinal spacing between consecutive generations is constant:

- `Delta(mu - e) = omega - omega/phi^2 = omega/phi ~ 38.83`
- `Delta(tau - mu) = omega/phi ~ 38.83`

Both jumps are exactly `omega/phi`. This means the tau's ordinal difference from the electron is exactly twice the muon's: `Delta_tau = 2 * Delta_mu`. The generation spacing is a single derived quantity.
