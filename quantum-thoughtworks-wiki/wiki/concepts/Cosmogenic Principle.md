# Cosmogenic Principle

The Cosmogenic Principle is the [[Matryoshka-Chronon Framework]]'s model for the origin of the universe. It states:

## The Process of Creation

1. **Undifferentiated Substrate**: Before creation, reality is featureless and symmetrical
2. **Contraction**: The [[Call of the Void]] potential (`V_void`) establishes a defined geometric landscape with a boundary condition at the equilibrium radius `r_eq`
3. **Impulse**: An initial informational impulse (quantum fluctuation) is injected into this potential landscape
4. **Toroidal Capture**: The impulse is drawn to the temporal horizon at `r_eq`, which has toroidal geometry -- a perfect engine for recursive information processing
5. **Generative Cascade**: The captured energy "shatters" into a fractal cascade of lower-order, stable, harmonic resonances

The nested [[Temporal Shells]], quantized particles, and laws of physics are the "shards" of this initial toroidal flow.
