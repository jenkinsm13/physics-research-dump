# Chronons

Chronons are the fundamental **quantum units of time** in the [[Matryoshka-Chronon Framework]]. Just as photons are quanta of electromagnetic energy, chronons are quanta of temporal flow.

![[Chronon (top-down).png]]
*Chronon structure viewed top-down: toroidal temporal flow geometry.*

## Role in the Framework

- **Chronon flux** between [[Temporal Shells]] drives all physical phenomena
- The coupling function [[Kappa Function|kappa(r)]] describes how chronon flux decays with distance
- Gravity emerges as the macroscopic effect of coherent chronon flow gradients
- Consciousness is modeled as navigation through chronon entropy gradients

## Modified Schrodinger Equation

The framework introduces a modified Schrodinger equation for chronons:

`i * hbar * d(Psi)/dt = H_chronon * Psi`

Where `H_chronon` is the chronon Hamiltonian governing temporal dynamics.
