# Temporal Shells

Temporal Shells are the fundamental structural units of reality in the [[Matryoshka-Chronon Framework]]. They are nested, concentric structures whose radii scale by powers of the [[Golden Ratio]] (`phi`):

`r_n = r_0 * phi^n`

![[temporal_shells.png]]
*Nested temporal shell structure: inner shells represent future, outer shells represent past. Gravity emerges as inverse temporal flow from outer to inner shells.*

## Properties

- **Self-similar**: The pattern at any scale mirrors the pattern at every other scale (fractal/holographic)
- **Discrete**: Reality is quantized into distinct shells, not continuous
- **Coupled**: Shells interact through [[Chronons|chronon flux]], which drives all physical phenomena
- **Grouped into domains**: Approximately `omega ~ 20*pi ~ 63` shells form one coherent quantum domain

## Physical Interpretation

Particles are **standing waves** (stable resonant modes) within the shell hierarchy. A particle's identity is determined by its [[Ordinal Depth]] -- the shell number where its wave-function is centered.

The universe requires approximately **294 shells** total, organized into `sqrt(21) ~ 4.58` independent quantum domains:
`294 ~ omega * sqrt(21)`

The number 21 is the 8th Fibonacci number, linking universal structure to the framework's octant-based reality model.
