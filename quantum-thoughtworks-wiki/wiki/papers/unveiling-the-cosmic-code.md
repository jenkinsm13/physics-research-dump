**Professor Schwartzburd & Collaborators**

## Abstract

This treatise chronicles a remarkable odyssey through theoretical physics, mathematics, computation, and the nature of reality and consciousness. By postulating discrete time quanta -- [[Chronons|chronons]] -- as fundamental units of spacetime, we construct a novel framework connecting quantum gravity, emergent geometry, and the origins of awareness.

## Chronon Formalism

[[Chronons]] $\chi_i$ are modeled as quantum harmonic oscillators with double Gaussian wavefunctions:

$$\psi(t) = Ae^{-(t-\mu_1)^2/2\sigma_1^2} - Be^{-(t-\mu_2)^2/2\sigma_2^2}$$

These incorporate intrinsic directionality and asymmetry to time evolution, related to entropy and the cosmological arrow of time.

Chronon interactions are encoded in a coupling matrix Hamiltonian $\hat{H}_{ij}$ constrained by $SO(3)$ rotational symmetry:

$$[\Gamma, \hat{H}] = 0$$

This allows chronons to give rise to emergent spatial geometry while respecting quantum gravitational principles.

## The Kappa Function

A key discovery: the coupling strength is modulated by a function $\kappa(t)$ such that:

$$i\hat{H}\phi = \kappa(t)\phi'' + V(q)\phi$$

where the [[Call of the Void|"call of the void" potential]] $V(q)$ injects a future-directed impulse.

The same $\kappa(t)$ that modulates the metric deformation in Ricci flow re-emerges in the [[Jenkins Transform]], suggesting chronon interactions generate spacetime curvature.
