## 3. Preferred Identity: $Z'$ Boson

The $d/c$ family at integer $n$ produces the tau (lepton), Z (neutral vector boson), and then the $n=4$ prediction. The natural continuation is a **heavy neutral vector boson** — a $Z'$:

- Same quantum numbers as the Z (spin-1, neutral, couples to fermions)
- Extends the electroweak sector naturally
- The $d/c$ amplitude is shared with the Z, suggesting the same coupling structure
- A scalar at $n = 4 + \pi$ would mirror the Z $\to$ Higgs pattern at $n = 2 \to 2+\pi$

## 4. LHC Status

### Current Exclusions

The Sequential Standard Model (SSM) $Z'$ — which has the same couplings as the SM Z boson — is **excluded at 4.66 TeV** by ATLAS and CMS dilepton searches.

### What Is NOT Excluded

Several well-motivated $Z'$ models remain viable at 4.66 TeV:

| Model | Description | Status at 4.66 TeV |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| SSM $Z'$ | SM-strength couplings | **Excluded** |
| Reduced-coupling $Z'$ | $g'/g_Z < 1$ | **Not excluded** for $g'/g_Z \lesssim 0.5$ |
| $E_6$ $Z'$ ($\chi$, $\psi$, $\eta$) | GUT-motivated | **Not excluded** |
| Leptophobic $Z'$ | Suppressed dilepton coupling | **Not excluded** |
| 3rd-generation-preferring $Z'$ | Enhanced $t\bar{t}$, $b\bar{b}$ | **Not excluded** |

The SSM exclusion is model-dependent. The framework does not specify the coupling strength — only the mass.
