# A Quantum Dynamical Approach to the Riemann Hypothesis

**Michael Christopher Jenkins** -- Jenkins Transformations, Inc.

## Abstract

This document outlines a conceptual framework attempting to bridge quantum chaos and prime number theory, targeting the Riemann Hypothesis. It explores scale symmetry in quantum dynamical systems on $n$-dimensional tori, drawing parallels with the distribution of zeros of the Riemann zeta function. The proposed conjecture suggests a deep relationship between spontaneous symmetry breaking in quantum systems and the non-trivial zeros of $\zeta(s)$.

---

## Key Definitions

**Riemann Zeta Function.** For $\text{Re}(s) > 1$:

$$\zeta(s) = \sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{1}{k^s}$$

**Scale Symmetry in Quantum Dynamics.** Invariance of a system's Hamiltonian under scale transformations, implying self-similarity in the state space manifold -- a hallmark of quantum chaos.

## Approach

The framework explores quantum dynamics on the $n$-torus, seeking connections between:

- Scale symmetry breaking patterns in quantum systems
- Distribution of non-trivial zeros of $\zeta(s)$ on the critical line $\text{Re}(s) = 1/2$
- Self-similar structures in quantum chaotic dynamics
