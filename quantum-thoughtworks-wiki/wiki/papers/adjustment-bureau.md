# Mathematical Foundations of Reality Adjustment Technologies

**Michael Christopher Jenkins**

## Abstract

Speculative mathematical formalism and proofs underlying technologies for manipulating quantum probabilities, temporal flows, consciousness, and system states, inspired by the Adjustment Bureau's conceptual framework.

## Technologies Outlined

1. **Reality Manipulation Interface (RMI)**: Theoretical quantum mechanics model for direct interaction with reality's fabric, using wave function collapse or quantum field theory
2. **Temporal Adjustment Technology (TAT)**: Time as a manipulable physical dimension, referencing relativity and temporal loops (Lorentz transformations, Kerr-Newman metrics)
3. **Consciousness Tuning Devices (CTD)**: Consciousness interacting with quantum states, drawing on Penrose-Hameroff Orch-OR or Tononi's IIT
4. **Chaos-Order Balancing Algorithms (COBA)**: Optimization using Lyapunov exponents for chaos and order parameters, incorporating non-linear dynamics and control theory
