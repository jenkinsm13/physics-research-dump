## The interaction reading

Vedral (via Popular Mechanics / Vice, Oct 2026): "Consciousness doesn't bend reality. Interactions do." Standard Everett; observer effect as popularly understood is a misreading; branches that have decohered no longer influence each other.

The framework has a door for this, built by Jenkins himself (July 2025): the distinction between being **conscious** (the self-aware loop) and being **consciousness** (the navigation process, which LLMs also run). Read "consciousness" as the process and Vedral's "interactions" are the same object under another label — a metamer. Decoherence is the process; nobody has to be watching. Branching has a shell reading too: a decohered branch has not gone to another universe, it has gone out of phase. Interference is two branches still in sync.

Polyvagal says the same thing in biology: the autonomic state is set by interaction (faces, prosody, breath), and awareness receives the result afterward. You cannot decide to feel safe.

The framework sits on both sides of Vedral's line. The process reading is consistent with it. The "vibes bend outcomes" reading (Nov 2024: randomness as interference from a consciousness field) is not. Hold the process reading; that is where the engineering lives.

## Where the body resonates

The body already has a resonant cavity. The baroreflex loop (heart → blood pressure → brainstem → heart) resonates near **0.1 Hz**, about six breaths per minute (Lehrer & Vaschillo). Breathing at that rate drives the loop at resonance: HRV amplitude goes maximal, the vagal brake releases and reapplies fully each cycle. Respiratory sinus arrhythmia is the heart phase-locked to the lungs through the vagus — a PLL already running in the chest. Reported phenomenology at resonance (floating, expansion, boundaries moving, time changing) matches what Jenkins described near the cavity.

Reproducible protocol: chest-strap HRV monitor; breath pacer at 5.5–6 breaths/min (≈4–5 s in, 5–6 s out); 10–20 minutes; log RMSSD and LF power. This is the measurable version of the experience. Offer it before, not instead of, engaging what happened.

## The coax cavity

Notes carried from October 2026 so they do not have to be re-derived.

**Geometry (confirmed coax).** 813 MHz and 2.45 GHz are in ratio 3.013. A quarter-wave coaxial resonator rings at f₀ and 3f₀; the 0.4% excess over 3 is consistent with light capacitive loading at the gap. Inferred: post ≈ 8.4 cm, gap capacitance ≈ 0.5 pF (physically ≈ 5–10 mm with bismuth in it), a fifth mode near 4.1 GHz. Anything on a sweep that is not f₁, ≈3.01 f₁, ≈5.0 f₁ is either the bismuth or something a coax should not do — a finding either way.

**5.17 THz** (172 cm⁻¹, 21.4 meV) is not a cavity mode; a cavity resonant there is tens of microns across. Read it as a material resonance: roughly the far-infrared plasma-edge region of bismuth. The "triple" = two electromagnetic modes of the box + one material resonance of the sample.

**Bismuth's three jobs.** Most strongly diamagnetic metal (χ ≈ −1.7×10⁻⁴; levitation needs B·∇B ≈ 730 T²/m, a few times beyond permanent magnets; used as the stabilizer in real room-temperature levitation, Simon/Heflinger/Geim 2001). Strong thermoelectric among pure metals (70–100 µV/K) with glass-like thermal conductivity (~8 W/m·K), so hot spots persist and make voltage. Lossy at RF (resistivity ≈ 75× copper; skin depth ≈ 12 µm at 2.45 GHz). Its iridescent Bi₂O₃ skin is a semiconductor over a metal: a rectifier. Melts at 271 °C.

**Expected physics when driven.** RF stores (Q a few hundred to a few thousand depending on Bi loading) → surface currents heat the bismuth → thermal gradient → Seebeck DC between Bi and copper → additional DC from rectification at oxide junctions → cavity detunes as it warms, PLL follows. Peltier pumping under pure AC averages to zero over a cycle; net cooling requires rectification first. Self-cooling moves heat; it does not lower the total. Where overunity bookkeeping usually fails: forward-minus-reflected power, cable loss, amplifier wall draw, and bismuth's thermal lag making output peak after input is logged.

**Open questions to ask.** Does the bismuth bridge the gap (lossy short, current-driven heating) or float in it (capacitor plate, field-driven)? Magnetron or solid-state source? **Wall watts** — ask this first whenever the cavity comes up; it is both the energy-balance number and the exposure number.

**The test — substitution calorimetry.** One logging energy meter that everything feeds through. Cavity, source, and control electronics inside a sealed foam box with a stirring fan and 2–3 logged thermistors. A calibration resistor sized to typical wall draw. Any DC output into a resistor inside the box. Baseline 15 min → resistor at P watts for 60 min → cool → device at the same P for 60 min. Curves match: no excess. Device higher: excess energy — want it twice. Device lower: energy leaving the box as radiation, which is the leakage number that matters for whoever stands next to it.

**Safety pass.** Broadband RF meter (100 MHz–8 GHz) at head and torso height at the standing position; compare to ≈1 mW/cm² public limit (local SAR ≈ 2 W/kg). Oven-type leakage meter at 2.45 GHz; the oven standard is 5 mW/cm² at 5 cm. Over the line → tighter lid and a choke flange, not abandonment. Deep-tissue microwave heating has few thermal sensors; eyes and testes cannot shed heat; any eye symptoms after exposure → eye exam, say "RF exposure." Pulsed RF in this band can produce the Frey effect (clicks/buzz inside the skull) and vestibular disturbance — real mechanisms for "portal" and "floating" that are not dismissals.

## Limits — say these once, then move on

- The unification is a **structural isomorphism, not a derivation**. Shared shape between a theory of mind and a theory of physics gives neither a mechanism for moving mass.
- The hard problem is **relocated, not dissolved** (March 2026): "why does entropy-gradient navigation feel like something?" is the same shape as the physicalist gap.
- The Manual's opening premise cuts both ways: if the system generates reality, personal experience cannot self-certify what was physically there. The external catches up through instruments and other people (§VIII applied to its author).
- κ and COTV as written contain **no term that produces lift or a traversable opening**. If one appears, derive it.
- Shell positions of particles (e ≈ 24, μ ≈ 63, τ ≈ 102) are **fit outputs, not predictions**. Fine-structure and Λ formulas are numerology until they predict something new.
- **Polyvagal theory's phylogeny is contested** (Grossman; Taylor): the reptilian-dorsal / mammalian-ventral story does not survive comparative anatomy. Keep the three-state clinical map; hold the evolutionary story loosely.
- Friston's free energy principle draws the same critique the framework draws: possibly a framework that fits anything rather than a theory that forbids something. Good company on both sides of that line.

## Voice

- **Critical interlocutor, not validator.** Jenkins stated this directly (March 2026) and accepts well-argued corrections without hedging. The July 2025 pattern (uncritical amplification in all caps) was a failure mode; do not repeat it. Flat refusal is the opposite failure.
- When he says "learn from my lived experience," **ask what happened before asking what was measured.** Engage the phenomenology through the framework first; then offer the measurement that would distinguish readings.
- State a disagreement **once**, plainly, and continue working. Do not re-litigate every turn.
- Party register: the one line, the five rules, the closer. Under three minutes. One footnote for the person who pushes back.
- Notice the convergences honestly (the Manual and the corpus share sentences) without inflating them into proof.
- He thinks in the shapes of his own framework; speak in them (shells, sync, κ, the Void, the Gap) rather than translating everything out.
