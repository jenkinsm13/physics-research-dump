## 1. The two objects, and the gap between them

Note 22 (§3) established the **finite** regulated vacuum energy **per stack**,
$$E_0=\sum_{n=0}^\infty \tfrac12\hbar\omega_n\,\kappa(+n)
=\tfrac12\hbar\omega_0\sum_{n=0}^\infty\varphi^n\,\mathrm{osc}(n)\,e^{-n/\varphi}
=5.41196\cdot\tfrac12\hbar\omega_0,$$
convergent because $\ln\varphi=0.481<1/\varphi=0.618$ (per-shell ratio $\varphi e^{-1/\varphi}=0.872<1$;
the sum is evaluated in log space, $\varphi^n e^{-n/\varphi}=e^{n(\ln\varphi-1/\varphi)}$, since
$\varphi^n$ alone overflows long before the decay bites). This is an **energy**, in units of the
un-anchored half-quantum $\tfrac12\hbar\omega_0$.

Note 08 supplies a **density**: the observed cosmological constant
$\Lambda\simeq1.1\times10^{-52}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}$ corresponds to a dark-energy **mass** density
$$\rho_\Lambda^{\rm mass}=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{8\pi G}
=\frac{(1.1\times10^{-52})(2.99792458\times10^8)^2}{8\pi(6.6743\times10^{-11})}
=5.894\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}},$$
i.e. an energy density
$$\rho_\Lambda=\rho_\Lambda^{\rm mass}c^2=5.297\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$$
(the textbook $\sim6\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J/m^3}$). **This is the keystone: one is an energy, the
other a density.** To equate them I need a **volume**, i.e. a length $r_0$. So $\Lambda$ alone pins
one *combination* of $(\omega_0,r_0)$ — it cannot separate them without one more relation. That
relation must come from the framework's own physics, not from a fitted mass; otherwise the route
is circular.

## 3. Route B2 — close with the framework's own dispersion

The spine carries **two** golden ladders locked together: frequencies $\omega_n=\omega_0\varphi^n$
and radii $r_n=r_0\varphi^n$ (note 00, note 22 §1). The relation between the seed frequency and the
seed length is **fixed by the boost group, not chosen**. The chronon two-vector advances one proper
interval $1/\varphi$ per shell (note 10: rapidity $\theta=k/\varphi$, $\|V\|^2=1$), and the proper
interval of a *null* light-crossing of the seed cell is $r_0/c$ in time and $r_0$ in length. Locking
the two ladders means one shell of frequency advance ($\omega_0$, the inverse seed period) equals one
light-crossing of the seed length: the seed phase velocity is the invariant speed,
$$\boxed{\;\omega_0\,r_0=c\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\omega_0=\frac{c}{r_0}\;}$$
This is forced: $c$ is the *only* speed the boost group $\Lambda(a)\Lambda(b)=\Lambda(a{+}b)$ leaves
invariant (it is the asymptote of $\beta=\tanh\theta$, note 09), so the seed cell — the $k=0$ rung
where $G_{\rm flow}=0$ and $G_{\rm sym}=\tfrac12$ (note 10) — can carry no other characteristic
velocity. Any other power $\omega_0\propto r_0^{-p}$ with $p\neq1$ would smuggle in a second invariant
speed $c\,(r_0/\ell)^{1-p}$, which the closed one-parameter boost group forbids. $p=1$ is therefore
not a convenient choice but the unique fixed point. With one stack occupying the seed volume $\sim r_0^3$, the regulated vacuum
**density** is
$$\rho_{\rm vac}=\frac{E_0\cdot\tfrac12\hbar\omega_0}{r_0^3}
=\frac{E_0}{2}\,\frac{\hbar c}{r_0^4}.$$
Setting $\rho_{\rm vac}=\rho_\Lambda$ gives **one equation in one unknown**:
$$\boxed{\;r_0^4=\frac{E_0}{2}\,\frac{\hbar c}{\rho_\Lambda}
=\frac{2.70598\times(3.16153\times10^{-26}\,\mathrm{J\,m})}{5.297\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}}
=1.615\times10^{-16}\ \mathrm{m^4}.\;}$$
Hence
$$\boxed{\;r_0=1.127\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m}\ (\approx0.11\ \mathrm{mm}),\qquad
\omega_0=\frac{c}{r_0}=2.660\times10^{12}\ \mathrm{rad/s}\ (\approx0.42\ \mathrm{THz}).\;}$$
This is **Route B's headline scale**: a *macroscopic* chronon ($\sim$0.1 mm) and a **THz** seed
frequency. The half-quantum is $\tfrac12\hbar\omega_0=8.75\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{eV}$ and
$\hbar\omega_0=1.751\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV}$. Strikingly, $\omega_0$ is **nowhere near**
Planckian — the measured smallness of $\Lambda$ pushes the seed scale far into the infrared, which
is precisely the diagnostic the electron test now exploits.

## 4. The electron test (the falsifiable post-diction)

The electron is the $k_e=24$ shell quantum (note 04). Its mass-energy is the shell energy
$\hbar\omega_{24}=\hbar\omega_0\varphi^{24}$ — **read with the $\omega_0$ pinned by $\Lambda$
above, with no lepton-fit intercept smuggled in** (using the fitted $b$ would be the forbidden
circularity). With $\varphi^{24}=1.03682\times10^5$ (exactly $L_{24}=103682$ to four figures),
$$E_e^{\rm pred}=\hbar\omega_0\,\varphi^{24}
=(1.751\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV})(1.03682\times10^5)=181.5\ \mathrm{eV}
=1.815\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{MeV}.$$
Compared to the measured $m_e c^2=0.510999\ \mathrm{MeV}$:
$$\boxed{\;\frac{E_e^{\rm pred}}{m_e c^2}=\frac{181.5\ \mathrm{eV}}{510999\ \mathrm{eV}}
=3.55\times10^{-4},\qquad \log_{10}=-3.45.\;}$$
**The prediction is $\sim2800\times$ too light — short by $3.4$ orders of magnitude.** Equivalently,
at Route B's $\omega_0$ the electron would have to sit at ordinal
$$k_{\rm needed}=\frac{\ln\!\big(m_e c^2/\hbar\omega_0\big)}{\ln\varphi}=40.5,$$
not $24$ — about **16.5 golden shells deeper** than the corpus places it. The miss is not a few
percent; it is structural.

## 5. Cross-check: the two ω₀ disagree by 3.4 orders

Invert the electron condition to find the $\omega_0$ the lepton ladder *demands*:

\qquad\frac{\omega_0^{(B2)}}{\omega_0^{(A)}}=3.55\times10^{-4}\ (\log_{10}=-3.45).$$

The disagreement is **weakly sensitive** to the one soft assumption — the stack volume power. With

load-bearing assumption, and it is the unique parameter-free one.

fudge.

The comparison the brief asks for: Route B gives $\omega_0\approx2.66\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{rad/s}$.
If Route A (thread 24, the mass-ladder/Dirac route) returns the electron-demanded

## Extension — the gap measured in inversion-shell units (a flagged near-integer coincidence)

The $3.4$-order disagreement could be a meaningless mismatch, or it could be *structured*. The
disciplined test is to measure it against the framework's own quantum of ordinal depth, the
**inversion-shell spacing** $\Delta k=\pi/(2\ln\varphi)=3.26425$ (corpus: the comb
$k_n=(2n{-}1)\pi/(4\ln\varphi)$). The electron needs to sit at $k_{\rm need}=40.51$ when the seed is
pinned by $\Lambda$, but the corpus places it at $k_e=24$. The displacement is
$$\boxed{\;\Delta k_{\Lambda\to e}=k_{\rm need}-k_e=16.506\ \text{shells}
=\frac{\Delta k_{\Lambda\to e}}{\Delta k}\,\Delta k
=5.057\,\Delta k.\;}$$
This is **close to** five inversion-shell spacings — $5\Delta k=16.321$ vs the required $16.506$, a
$1.1\%$ residual in shell-count. **I flag this as a near-integer coincidence, not an established
structure.** The honest weight is exactly that: *one* integer coincidence at the $\sim1\%$ level, with
**no independent confirmation** and **no a-priori reason the offset should be five** — I checked the
corpus's own ordinals (the generation step $\approx12\,\Delta k$, the comb shells $1.63/4.90/8.16$) and
found no relation that selects $5$. So this is a postdiction noted for follow-up, on equal footing with
how a coincidence-vs-structure ambiguity should always be reported, not a derivation. With that
caveat stated plainly, the arithmetic of the would-be offset is
$$\omega_0^{(A)}\overset{?}{=}\omega_0^{(B2)}\,\varphi^{\,5\Delta k}
=\omega_0^{(B2)}\,\varphi^{16.321}
=\omega_0^{(B2)}\times 2.575\times10^{3},$$
which gives $2.660\times10^{12}\times2.575\times10^{3}=6.85\times10^{15}$ — within $8.5\%$ of the
electron-demanded $7.49\times10^{15}$ (the gap is precisely the $0.057\,\Delta k$ non-integer part,
$\varphi^{0.057\,\Delta k}=1.093$). The speculative reading — explicitly labelled speculative — is that
the gravitating vacuum and the electron *might* be two terraces of one spine separated by five
inversions of the now-surface. **What would turn the coincidence into structure:** an independent route
(A or C) landing the matter seed at $\omega_0\sim6.85\times10^{15}$ (i.e. *exactly* $5\Delta k$ above
the vacuum seed, not merely "near $10^{16}$"), or a second, unrelated framework quantity whose offset
from the vacuum ladder is also an integer multiple of $\Delta k$. Absent either, the offset is reported
as what it is: a flagged $\sim1\%$ near-integer, riding on top of a clean, unambiguous negative result.
