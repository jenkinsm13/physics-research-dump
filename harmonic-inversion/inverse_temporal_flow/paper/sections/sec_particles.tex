\section{The particle sector}\label{sec:particles}

The two-vector $\Vvec(k)=(\cosh\rap,-\sinh\rap)$, $\rap=k/\phigr$, and its
oscillatory weight $\osc(k)=1/(1+\cos^2(2k\lnphi))$ are enough to read the
Standard-Model fermions off the flow-reversal lattice. The single spectroscopic
phase is $\theta(k)\equiv 2k\lnphi\pmod\pi$, and gravity, flow, and correlation
weight are all functions of it through $\osc(k)$. Bosons live on the
oscillation peaks $\osc=1$ (the inversion shells $k_n=(2n-1)\pi/(4\lnphi)$);
fermions live on the nodes $\osc=\half$, since the spin-$\half$ half-boost
$\rap/2=k/(2\phigr)$ at doubled depth lands $\osc(2k_n)=\half=\osc(0)$ exactly.
Half-integer spin thus inhabits the antisymmetric $\Gflow$ branch natively.

\subsection{Charged leptons: the $SO(2)$ rotor orbit}

The mass fit places the charged leptons at ordinals $k_e\approx 24$,
$k_\mu\approx 63$, $k_\tau\approx 102$ --- an arithmetic comb with exact integer
step
\begin{equation}
k_\mu-k_e=k_\tau-k_\mu=39=6\Dk \;\equiv\; \Delta_g,
\label{eq:particles:step}
\end{equation}
one generation being $39$ shells (six oscillation periods, twelve inversion-shell
terraces) deeper into inverse flow. The phase decrement is constant to machine
precision because $39$ shells advance the raw phase by $78\lnphi=11.948\,\pi$,
whose floor count of $\pi$-boundaries is exactly $12$ at every step. Folding out
those twelve flow reversals gives the generational drift invariant
\begin{equation}
\delta\theta_g \;=\; 78\lnphi-12\pi \;=\; -0.16459\ \mathrm{rad},
\label{eq:particles:drift}
\end{equation}
a theorem, not a fit. Lifting each lepton to its phase vector
$\mathbf e(k)=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$, the whole tower is one rigid rotation,
\begin{equation}
\mathbf e(k+\Delta_g)=\mathsf R(\delta\theta_g)\,\mathbf e(k),
\qquad \det\mathsf R=1,
\label{eq:particles:rotor}
\end{equation}
verified $\mathsf R\,\mathbf e(24)=\mathbf e(63)$, etc., to $\lesssim 10^{-14}$.
Since $\delta\theta_g/\pi$ is irrational the orbit never closes: the lepton comb
is aperiodic and Weyl-equidistributes. The three lepton ordinals are an exactly
equispaced (rigid) orbit; this rigidity is why there is no tree-level lepton
mixing. The next rung is exact and parameter-free, $k_4=k_\tau+\Delta_g=141$;
the mass there is only \emph{bracketed} to $\sim1.4$--$160$~GeV, as three points
cannot stably fix both the affine slope and the $\cos\theta$ curvature term.

\subsection{Spin and the half-boost}

The boost-invariant mass is the hyperbolic Casimir, identical for the vector and
its spinor half-boost:
\begin{equation}
M^2=\Gsym^2-\Gflow^2=\osc^2(k),
\qquad (\Gsym,-\Gflow)=\osc(k)\,(\cosh\rap,\sinh\rap),
\label{eq:particles:casimir}
\end{equation}
verified $M^2=\osc^2$ to $\le2.5\times10^{-14}$. The spin--torsion contact term
cancels out of $M^2$. On an inversion shell $M^2(k_n)=1$ while the local torsion
rate $\tfren$ passes through zero --- flow reversal at a unit mass-shell, not a
vanishing of coupling.

\subsection{Quarks and CKM (honest framing)}

Inverting the lepton calibration $\ln m=ak+b$ ($a=0.10454$) on the PDG masses
gives quark ordinals $u/d/s/c/b/t=37.8/45.2/73.8/98.8/110.2/145.8$ --- a
\emph{strained} rotor orbit, not equispaced. A mass ratio is a full boost chord
$m_i/m_j=e^{a(k_i-k_j)}$, so its square root is the spin-$\half$ half-boost
chord. The Cabibbo angle is the down-sector half-chord:
\begin{equation}
\lambda=\sin\theta_c \;\overset{\text{GST}}{\simeq}\; \sqrt{m_d/m_s}=0.2242,
\label{eq:particles:cabibbo}
\end{equation}
versus $|V_{us}|=0.2243$ ($0.0\%$), $\theta_c=12.96^\circ$ vs $13.04^\circ$. This
is the standard Gatto--Sartori--Tonin relation, and we are explicit that
\emph{$\phigr$ cancels identically}: substituting $k_i-k_j=(\ln m_i-\ln m_j)/a$
collapses the exponent to $\exp[\half(\ln m_i-\ln m_j)]$, so both $\lnphi$ and
$a$ drop out and $\lambda$ is a function of $m_d,m_s$ alone (confirmed to
$<3\times10^{-17}$). The framework owns the \emph{interpretation} --- generation
mixing is a half-boost chord on the inversion circle, making GST mandatory rather
than coincidental --- not the digit. The Wolfenstein ladder is reproduced in
structure but only bracketed numerically: $|V_{cb}|\sim\lambda^2$ is $+23\%$

\begin{equation}

\subsection{Electroweak gauge structure}

The flow-space $2\times2$ generators are already on the table. The spinor
half-boost $\sigma_x$, the now-involution $\sigma_z$, and their commutator
$\sigma_y$ close $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ \emph{exactly} (residual $0$),
$[\sigma_x,\sigma_y]=2i\sigma_z$ and cyclic --- so $SU(2)_L$ is the half-boost
triplet, acting only on the spinor (chiral) doublet while the full boost stays a
weak-isospin singlet. Hypercharge $U(1)_Y$ is the compact rotor of
\eqref{eq:particles:rotor}, the Wick sibling of the boost. Positing the
coupling-ratio ansatz $\tan^2\theta_W=1/\phigr$ (flagged as the load-bearing
assumption; the algebra fixes the generators, not their coupling normalization),
the golden identity $\phigr+1=\phigr^2$ gives an exact bare value, and one shell
quantum of running carries it to
\begin{equation}
\sin^2\theta_W(m_Z)=\frac{1}{\phigr^2}\cdot\frac{1}{\phigr}=\frac{1}{\phigr^3}

\label{eq:particles:weinberg}
\end{equation}
versus the measured $\overline{\text{MS}}$ value $0.23121$: $+2.1\%$. We flag the
scheme honestly --- on-shell the miss is $+5.8\%$, the same size as the

the table. The mass ratio $m_W/m_Z=\sqrt2/\phigr=0.8740$ is the exact tree-level

does not close from the $(1{+}1)$-dimensional flow-space --- recorded as an open

\subsection{Neutrinos: the near-null sector}

Neutrinos sit \emph{on} the osc-nodes $\osc=\half$ at $k^\nu_m=m\,\Dk=3.26425\,m$,

light'' story is rejected: it is only a factor $\tfrac23$ and overshoots the scale
by $\sim 10^2$. The real suppressor is the detailed-balance round-trip weight

$M_R\sim10^{10}$--$10^{14}$~GeV (mass-ladder ordinal $\sim330$, near the horizon
shell $\Nstar=291.88$). The $m=0$ node is exactly massless ($\Gflow(0)=0$),
forcing normal ordering $m_1=0<m_2<m_3$. The unified mass law is one formula,
texture from the node $\sinh$-ladder and scale from the seesaw:
\begin{equation}
m_\nu(m)=\sqrt{\Delta m^2_{32}}\;
\frac{\sinh(m\,\Dk/\phigr)}{\sinh(2\,\Dk/\phigr)},
\qquad
