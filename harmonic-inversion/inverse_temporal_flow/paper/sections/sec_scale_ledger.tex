\label{sec:scale}

\noindent\textbf{Routes A and C agree --- but trivially.} Both land on $\eP$ because both anchor on $\lP$: Route C reaches it through $G=\lP^2 c^3/\hbar$, which is an \emph{identity} once $r_0=\lP$. $G$ is therefore \emph{recovered, not predicted}, and $A=C$ is a consistency check, not the over-determination we hoped for.

\noindent\textbf{Route B disagrees by 23 orders of magnitude --- and that is informative.} The vacuum pins a different but real scale, $r_0\approx0.11$ mm, essentially the dark-energy length $\ell_{DE}=(\hbar c/\rho_\Lambda)^{1/4}\approx 85\,\mu$m. The ladder is thus anchored at \emph{both ends}: the Planck/UV end by geometry and gravity, the dark-energy/IR end by the vacuum, the two ends being the $\Nstar\approx292$ golden shells apart that the hierarchy always claimed. The ends pin the \emph{span}; whether they pin it \emph{consistently} remains near-definitional, since $\Nstar$ was itself defined from $R_H/\lP$.

\noindent\textbf{The matter rung is pinned by none of them.} This is the real finding. The electron sits at
\begin{equation}

\label{eq:scale:ne}
\end{equation}
\emph{not} a $\phigr$-lattice integer. The tempting closed forms were checked and \emph{disowned} by the audit: $\tfrac25\Nstar$ misses $m_e$ by $99\%$, and $\Nstar/e=107.38$ lands $0.30$ shell away (a $13\%$ mass miss) but $1/e$ has no low-denominator rational approximant --- numerology, not structure. The verify script was rewritten to \emph{assert the disownment} rather than reward the near-miss. The framework is, at root, a theory of dimensionless structure, one integer short of absolute masses:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\ln m_e = -n_e\,\lnphi + \ln \eP, \qquad n_e\approx 107\ \text{(the ``desert depth'') is the one missing integer.}\;}
\label{eq:scale:desert}
\end{equation}
