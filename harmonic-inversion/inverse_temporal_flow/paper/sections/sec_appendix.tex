\section{The corpus and its verification}\label{app:corpus}

This paper is a capstone compiled from $32$ companion notes that form the verified
research corpus behind the chronon two-vector. The notes are organized into a single
spine note ($00$), which fixes the conventions and the core construction $\Vvec(k)=(\cosh\rap,\,-\sinh\rap)$ with
$\rap=k/\phigr$; a first/second-round development layer ($01$--$15$) that branches the spine into
the individual derivations (the oscillation envelope $\osc(k)$, the inversion comb, the mass
shell $M^2=\Gsym^2-\Gflow^2=\osc^2$, the conserved current $\Ly=\|\Vvec\|^2$, and the
boost-composition law); a new-physics and keystone-thread layer ($16$--$28$) that carries the
results into particle masses, mixing angles, decay budgets, and the Planck-scale anchor; and
three closing index notes---a keystone verdict ($97$), a new-physics ledger ($98$), and a
synthesis index ($99$). Every note ships with a self-contained \texttt{python} verification
script under \texttt{verify/}. Each script was re-run independently for this compilation:
all numerical residuals returned at machine precision (typically $\le \sim 10^{-12}$, several
at the floating-point floor) and every script exited with status $0$. We preserve the corpus's
internal honesty grading throughout the body---results the ledger marks as an honest bracket or
an audit-demoted claim are reported as such here and are \emph{not} silently promoted to clean wins.

Table~\ref{tab:corpus:identities} collects a representative slice of the framework's
exact and near-exact checks together with their measured residuals. Entries marked ``exact''
hold algebraically; the remaining residuals are the worst-case deviations reported by the
corresponding \texttt{verify/} script. The two entries without a residual column value are
\emph{closed-form anchors}: $\sin^2\theta_W=1/\phigr^3$ and $\Lambda_\phigr=e^{\pi/2}$ are
exact statements of the framework, quoted at their numerical values for reference.

Two caveats travel with this table. First, the Planck anchor $\omega_0=\eP$ carries a residual
of $1.2\times 10^{-7}$ rather than machine precision; this is the propagated uncertainty of the
input constants ($\hbar$, $c$, $\lP$), not a defect of the identity, and the note records it as
such. Second, the decay-budget and density entries ($\Gamma_{\mathrm{tot}}$, $\nu$) are flagged
in the new-physics ledger ($98$) as near-exact correspondences rather than first-principles
derivations: they reproduce the quoted values to the digits shown, but the corpus does not
claim them as closed-form consequences of the two-vector axioms. The remaining identities in
the table---the conserved current, the mass shell at the inversion comb $k_n=(2n-1)\pi/(4\lnphi)$,
the boost group law, and the $\kappa$ asymmetry---are exact in the framework and verify to the
floating-point floor.
