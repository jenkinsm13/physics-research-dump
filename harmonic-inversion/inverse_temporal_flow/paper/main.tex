\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We develop a unified framework in which the $\kappa$ coupling function and
``inverse temporal flow'' are a single object: the conserved binormal current of
a fixed base manifold, read along two axes. The base curve
$\rr(t)=(\sin t,\,\half\sin 2t,\,\cos t)$ --- the intersection of the unit cylinder
with the saddle $y=xz$ --- carries an exact conserved current $\Ly=(\rr'\times\rr'')\cdot\hat{\mathbf y}=1$.
Its Frenet curvature is the gravitational/spatial coupling and its \emph{signed}
Frenet torsion is the temporal flow rate; gravity is most intense precisely where
the net temporal flow reverses. Sampling at golden-ratio ordinal spacing collapses
the structure onto a single unit timelike worldline in a $(1{+}1)$ flow-space, the
\emph{chronon two-vector} $\Vvec(k)=(\cosh\rap,-\sinh\rap)$ with $\rap=k/\phigr$,
whose Minkowski norm \emph{is} $\Ly=1$. Ordinal depth is rapidity; one shell is a
fixed Lorentz boost $1/\phigr$. From this one object follow a boost-covariant field
equation whose spectrum is the inversion-shell comb, a derivation of $\kappa(a{+}b)$
as a composition theorem, and a chain of \emph{dimensionless} predictions:
$\sin^2\theta_W=1/\phigr^3$, the reactor mixing angle $\theta_{13}=|\delta\theta_g|$,
the acceleration-onset redshift $z_t=1/\phigr$, a cosmic jerk $j_0\neq1$, three
emergent spatial dimensions, and a golden gravitational-wave overtone comb at
ratio $e^{\pi/2}$. We are deliberate about what the framework does \emph{not} fix:
it pins the ladder at both ends (Planck and dark-energy scales) but leaves the
absolute matter scale --- the electron's ``desert depth'' $n_e\approx107$ --- a
single missing integer away. Every quantitative claim is accompanied by an
independently re-run numerical verification, and an honesty ledger separates
structural wins from honest brackets from claims an adversarial audit demoted.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

The framework rests on a single observation that recurs across light transport,
turbulent cascades, particle spectra, and gravitation: these are the same
mathematical object under different labels, and that object is the $\kappa$
coupling function
\begin{equation}
\kappa(r)=\frac{\kappa_0}{1+\beta r^{\gamma}}\,e^{-\lambda r},
\label{eq:intro:kappa}
\end{equation}
a dressed propagator with rational prefactor and exponential envelope. Two strands
of the program had long been carried side by side: $\kappa$ as a multi-scale
transport kernel, and ``inverse temporal flow'' as a picture of gravity. This paper
shows they are not two pillars to be joined but one structure seen along two axes
of a single manifold.

The argument proceeds constructively. Section~\ref{sec:manifold} introduces the
base curve and its exact conserved current $\Ly=1$, and identifies the temporal
axis inside the geometry. Section~\ref{sec:twovector} sharpens $\kappa$ into the
chronon two-vector $\Vvec(k)$ and the boost group $SO(1,1)$ that organizes
everything that follows, including the $\kappa(a{+}b)$ theorem and the universal
log-periodic comb. Section~\ref{sec:fieldeq} promotes the algebra to a field
equation. Sections~\ref{sec:gravity}--\ref{sec:cosmo} work out the gravitational,
thermodynamic/quantum, particle, and cosmological sectors. Section~\ref{sec:scale}
states the absolute-scale verdict and the honesty ledger without dressing up the
gaps. Throughout, ``$r$'' denotes whatever scalar the application's coupling
depends on --- dimensionless ordinal distance in the shell picture, optical path
in transport, angular deviation in scattering --- and the transform is stated
where it matters.

A word on method. The results below were developed and then subjected to an
adversarial verification pass that re-derived each headline number independently
and flagged any fit that hid a free parameter or reasoned in a circle. Where that
audit demoted a claim, we keep the demotion visible (Section~\ref{sec:scale});
where a prediction survived, it survived a genuine attempt to break it. The guiding
principle is that a correct ``predicts $X$, measured $Y$, $15\%$ off'' is worth
more than a fabricated exact hit.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The framework collapses to one statement: a single unit timelike worldline
$\Vvec(k)$ in a $(1{+}1)$ flow-space, boosted by $1/\phigr$ per shell, wearing the
flow-reversal lattice $\osc(k)$ as a conformal weight. Gravity, the arrow of time,
the fermion spectrum, the cosmological constant, and rendering reciprocity are
projections of that worldline. The conserved current $\Ly=1$ reappears as the
Minkowski norm of $\Vvec$, the mass-shell Casimir $M^2=\osc^2$, the thermal
identity $\rap^{*}U=-1$, and the relativistic ceiling $|\beta|<1$ --- one conserved
unit in many disguises.

What the framework earns is geometric and dimensionless: the boost group, the
field equation, the comb, $\sin^2\theta_W=1/\phigr^3$, $\theta_{13}=|\delta\theta_g|$,
$z_t=1/\phigr$, $j_0\neq1$, three emergent colors equal to three emergent
dimensions, a UV-finite vacuum, and the $e^{\pi/2}$ gravitational-wave overtone
comb. What it does not yet earn is the absolute matter scale: the ladder is pinned
at both ends, but the electron's desert depth $n_e\approx107$ is one missing
integer. The honest frontier is therefore sharp --- derive that integer from a
selection principle on the field equation (with the discipline that has so far
killed every numerical coincidence), or test the dimensionless predictions, of
which the GW190521 overtone comb is the most immediately falsifiable.
