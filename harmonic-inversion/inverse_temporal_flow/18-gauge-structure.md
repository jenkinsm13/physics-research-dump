## 1. The gauge inventory is already on the table

We do not need to *postulate* a gauge group; the boost group of note 10 has
handed us four structures, and the Standard-Model electroweak factor
$SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$ is assembled from exactly two of them, with the now-surface

$$
K=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}\ (\text{boost, 10}),\quad
\sigma_x=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\ (\text{half-boost, 07}),\quad
\sigma_z=T=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}\ (\text{now-involution, 14}),\quad

with $K^2=\sigma_x^2=\sigma_z^2=I$ and $J^2=-I$ (all residual $0$). The boost $K$
is the *gravitational* channel and stays out of the gauge group — it is gravity,

## 2. $SU(2)_L$ is the spinor half-boost triplet — and it closes exactly

Note 07 gave the spinor sector its own generator, the half-boost $\sigma_x$, with

$$
\boxed{\;[\sigma_x,\sigma_y]=2i\,\sigma_z,\quad

[\sigma_z,\sigma_x]=2i\,\sigma_y,\qquad

This is the structural payoff: **$SU(2)_L$ is generated by {spinor half-boost
