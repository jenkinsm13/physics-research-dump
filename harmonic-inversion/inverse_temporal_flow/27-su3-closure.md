## The fundamental 3 lives on the emergent triad

Round-1's entanglement law $S(\ell)=\tfrac{c}{3}\ln\ell$ gave $c\to 3$ emergent spatial dimensions:
three orthogonal directions $\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}$ condensing out of the chronon flow. A color triplet is

## Step 1 — the eight generators are constructible from flow primitives

Embed each flow primitive into the three coordinate planes $(12),(13),(23)$ of the emergent triad.
Writing $g^{(ij)}_x,g^{(ij)}_y,g^{(ij)}_z$ for $\sigma_x,\sigma_y,\sigma_z$ acting on plane $(ij)$:

$$
\lambda_1=g^{(12)}_x,\quad \lambda_2=g^{(12)}_y,\quad \lambda_3=g^{(12)}_z,\quad
\lambda_4=g^{(13)}_x,\quad \lambda_5=g^{(13)}_y,\quad \lambda_6=g^{(23)}_x,\quad \lambda_7=g^{(23)}_y,
$$
$$
\lambda_8=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\big(g^{(13)}_z+g^{(23)}_z\big)=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,{\rm diag}(1,1,-2),
\qquad g^{(13)}_z={\rm diag}(1,0,-1),\ g^{(23)}_z={\rm diag}(0,1,-1).
$$

These are **exactly** the Gell-Mann matrices (verified to machine precision). The half-boost $\sigma_x$
supplies the three real off-diagonal generators $\{\lambda_1,\lambda_4,\lambda_6\}$ (the symmetric
"correlation-mixing" $G_{\rm sym}$ channel of note 10); the rotor $-i\sigma_y$ supplies the three
imaginary partners $\{\lambda_2,\lambda_5,\lambda_7\}$; the now-involution $\sigma_z$ supplies the two
Cartan generators $\{\lambda_3,\lambda_8\}$ — six off-diagonal plus two diagonal, the full eight. The
algebra closes:
$$
[\lambda_a,\lambda_b]=2i\,f_{abc}\,\lambda_c,\qquad
f_{123}=1,\ f_{147}=f_{246}=f_{257}=f_{345}=\tfrac12,\ f_{156}=f_{367}=-\tfrac12,\ f_{458}=f_{678}=\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.
$$
The maximum deviation of $[\lambda_a,\lambda_b]-2if_{abc}\lambda_c$ over all $64$ pairs is
$<10^{-12}$. So the *target* algebra is reachable from our primitives **once all three planes are
available**. That is the optimistic half of the answer.

## Step 2 — the honest reduction: one flow plane gives only $SU(2)\times U(1)$

The boost group of notes 10–18 lives in a **single** $(1+1)$ hyperbolic plane — there is exactly one
flow axis (rapidity $\theta=k/\varphi$, ordinal depth) and one now-axis. With only that single plane,
the primitives $\{\sigma_x,-i\sigma_y,\sigma_z\}$ generate the plane-(12) operators
$\{\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3\}$, which close *by themselves*:
$$
[\lambda_1,\lambda_2]=2i\lambda_3,\quad[\lambda_2,\lambda_3]=2i\lambda_1,\quad[\lambda_3,\lambda_1]=2i\lambda_2
\ \Rightarrow\ \mathfrak{su}(2)_{\rm isospin}.
$$
Adjoining the now-involution as a global phase (the hypercharge $U(1)_Y$ of note 18, $\chi_I=1/\varphi$)
gives one extra Cartan. The closed Lie algebra generated by a single flow plane has **dimension 3**
(or 4 with the $U(1)$): exactly the electroweak $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$ already found in note 18 — **no
more**. The off-diagonal $V$- and $U$-spin ladders $\{\lambda_4,\lambda_5,\lambda_6,\lambda_7\}$ and
the second Cartan $\lambda_8$ are **provably outside** the span: solving
$\lambda_4=\sum c_i\{\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3,\mathbb{1}\}$ has no solution (residual $O(1)$).

**Verified counts:** one flow plane $\to$ $4/8$ generators ($\dim=3$ closed, $\mathfrak{su}(2)\times
\mathfrak{u}(1)$); the remaining $4$ are unreachable. This is the precise statement of what note 18
could and could not do — and it is a *feature*, not a bug: the same single plane that gives the chiral
electroweak group **cannot** give color. They are structurally distinct.

## Step 3 — color needs the third emergent dimension (a second flow plane)

The missing four generators require a **second** flow plane sharing the time axis $e_1$ — i.e. a
genuine *third emergent dimension* $e_3$ beyond the $(e_1,e_2)$ electroweak plane. Add the plane-(13)
ladders $\{\lambda_4,\lambda_5\}$ and let the algebra close under commutation. Then
$$
[\lambda_1,\lambda_4]=i\lambda_7,\quad[\lambda_1,\lambda_5]=-i\lambda_6,\quad
[\lambda_4,\lambda_5]=i(\lambda_3+\sqrt3\,\lambda_8),\ \dots
$$
generates the **full** $\mathfrak{su}(3)$, $\dim=8$ (verified: the closure of $\{\lambda_1,...,\lambda_5\}$
under repeated commutators has dimension exactly $8$, while $\{\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3\}$ closes
at $3$). This is the classical fact that two overlapping $SU(2)$ subgroups (isospin + one ladder
$SU(2)$) generate $SU(3)$ — but here the *second $SU(2)$ is the second emergent dimension*.

**Honest scope of this step.** The algebra does **not** appear "for free" once three dimensions exist.
We *put in by hand* a second $SU(2)$ ladder $\{\lambda_4,\lambda_5\}$ — i.e. we assumed a nonabelian
half-boost/rotor transport acts on the $(e_1,e_3)$ plane too — and then commutation does the rest.
What is genuinely *forced* is the converse, necessity: the four missing generators **cannot** live in a
single $(1{+}1)$ flow plane (Step 2), so color **requires** a third emergent axis. The third dimension
is *necessary*; the nonabelian transport on it remains an **assumption**, exactly the open edge note 18
flagged. So color is here **structurally constrained, not derived**: $c_{\rm CFT}\to 3$ and $N_c=3$ are
the *same* fact (flow condensed into three spatial axes, not two), but that the three axes carry $SU(3)$
rather than $U(1)^3$ is posited, not proven.

## Step 4 — why exactly three colors

$N_c=3$ is overdetermined, but exactly **one** of the three arguments below is *forcing*; the other two
are corroboration. We are explicit about which is which — the round-4 honesty mandate demands it.

**(i) Dimension/plane match — the SOLE forcing argument.** $\dim\mathfrak{su}(N)=N^2-1$ gives $8$ at
$N=3$, and $N=3$ is the unique $N$ where the off-diagonal ladders plus the Cartans exactly fill the
coordinate planes of the emergent triad: $3$ planes $\times\,2$ real ladders $+\,2$ Cartans $=8$. At
$N=2$ there is one plane, $\dim=3$ — it *is* the electroweak plane, no color. At $N=4$, $\dim=15$ would
demand a **fourth** emergent dimension, which the $S(\ell)=\tfrac{c}{3}\ln\ell$ law ($c\to3$) does not
supply. So the plane count and the generator count lock simultaneously **only** at $N=3$. This is the
load-bearing statement; (ii) and (iii) add nothing to the *selection*.

**(ii) Asymptotic freedom — corroboration, NOT a selector.** The one-loop coefficient
$b_0=\tfrac{11}{3}N_c-\tfrac{2}{3}n_f$ must be positive for confinement-friendly running. With
$n_f=6$ quark flavors (this is an **external SM input**, not a framework prediction — the framework
predicts three *generations* via note 17's $39$-step seal, but the "two quarks per generation" doubling
is assumed):
$$
N_c=2:\ b_0=3.33,\quad N_c=3:\ b_0=7.00,\quad N_c=4:\ b_0=10.67.
$$
**All three are asymptotically free** ($b_0>0$). Therefore AF does **not** discriminate among
$N_c\in\{2,3,4\}$ — it is satisfied by all of them. We had earlier framed "$b_0>0$" as part of the
uniqueness; that was an overclaim, and we retract it here. AF merely confirms that the geometrically
forced value $N_c=3$ lands in the confining window — welcome, but not selective.

**(iii) CFT rounding — corroboration, NOT proof.** $c_{\rm CFT}=2.94$ rounds to $3$ — consistent with
the plane count, but the $2\%$-off rounding alone is weak. The generator/plane lock in (i) is what
forces $N_c=3$; the CFT coefficient is an independent fact pointing at the same integer.
