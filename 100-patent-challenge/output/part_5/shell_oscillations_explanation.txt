SHELL-MODIFIED NEUTRINO OSCILLATIONS VISUALIZATION
=================================================

These visualizations show how the Matryoshka shell framework modifies neutrino oscillation probabilities.

In standard quantum mechanics, neutrino oscillation probability is given by:
P(νₐ → νᵦ) = |∑ⱼ Uₐⱼ* Uᵦⱼ exp(-i·Δmⱼ²·L/(2E))|²

In the shell framework, this is modified to:
P(νₐ → νᵦ) = |∑ⱼ Uₐⱼ* Uᵦⱼ exp(-i·Δmⱼ²·L/(2E)·Γ(j,ω))|²

Where Γ(j,ω) = 1 + λᵥ·|j-ω/2|/ω is the shell-structure modification term.

Key observations from these visualizations:

1. The shell modification preserves the overall oscillation pattern but changes the exact location and amplitude of oscillation maxima.

2. The relative effect of shell modifications ranges from -5% to +5% depending on energy and baseline.

3. The shell effect is most pronounced at specific energy-baseline combinations where interference between different mass eigenstates is maximized.

4. The pattern of shell modifications follows a distinctive structure that could be detected in precise measurements from long-baseline neutrino experiments.

This effect emerges directly from the first principles of the Matryoshka shell framework, where neutrinos are treated as excitations of the shell structure at specific ordinal depths.

The shell parameter λᵥ = 0.014233 and ω = 63.32 used in these calculations come directly from the golden ratio structure of the shell framework.
