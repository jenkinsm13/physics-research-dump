## Delivery Notes

### Voice Modulation
- **Mathematical sections**: Slower, precise articulation
- **Vision sections**: Increased pace and energy
- **Validation points**: Authoritative, matter-of-fact tone

### Physical Presence
- Maintain steady eye contact during key claims
- Use hand gestures to illustrate shell structures
- Stand still during equation explanations, move during transitions

### Objection Handling
- **"No PhD?"** → "Revolutionary physics requires revolutionary thinking"
- **"Too ambitious?"** → "The mathematics is conservative; only the implications are revolutionary"
- **"Why you?"** → "Because institutional thinking created these problems—independent thinking solves them"

### Closing Power Phrase
"The universe has been waiting billions of years for conscious beings to understand its true nature. That wait ends today."
