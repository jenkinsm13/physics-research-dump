1. **Core Physics Layer**: Mathematical models of temporal dynamics

4. **Application Layer**: Practical devices based on the framework

## Important Implementation Details

- All spatial coordinates are derived from temporal coordinates using the kappa function
- The framework uses a "time-first" approach where space emerges from time
- Shell indices represent different temporal layers, not spatial distances
- Chronon flux between shells drives all physical phenomena
- The Call of the Void potential creates gravitational effects through shell distortion
